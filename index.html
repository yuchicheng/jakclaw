Jak Claw Assistant

Jak Claw Assistant is a personal AI assistant run by Yu-Chi (Jack) Cheng for his own use. It works through its own Google account, jakclaw.assistant@gmail.com, to send its own email, put events on Jack’s calendar as invitations, and organise files in Google Drive folders shared with it.

It is not a public service and has no other users.
